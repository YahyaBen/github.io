Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains how Hyrkul (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you use Maps Carburant FR (“the Application”). It covers the current Application, including its optional device features and component examples.

Privacy contact: yahya.ben.abdallah@gmail.com

1. Information we access and why

The current Application does not require an account. The information handled depends on the features you choose to use:

  • Location. If you request your current location in Settings and grant permission, the Application reads your device’s approximate or precise location to display it to you. The current location feature keeps the result in memory; it does not store a location history or upload your coordinates to a Hyrkul backend. The Application does not request background location access.
  • Camera and photos. When you take or select photos in the photo examples, the Application accesses the images you choose to display them on your device. These images are not uploaded to a Hyrkul backend. The system camera or photo picker controls the capture or selection.
  • Microphone permission. The current Application includes a microphone permission control in Settings. Granting that permission does not start a recording. The current Application does not record or upload audio.
  • Notifications. The Application may ask for notification permission. In Android builds configured for remote notifications, registration can create device and push tokens processed by Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. Development builds may also join a testing notification topic.
  • Preferences. Language, appearance, reduced-motion choices and device-feature preferences are stored locally to remember your settings.
  • Diagnostics. Builds containing Expo Observe can send startup and navigation performance measurements and diagnostic events to Expo. These can include app and operating-system information, technical identifiers, event timestamps and timing measurements. We use them to understand reliability and troubleshoot problems. Network services also receive technical request information, such as your IP address, when your device connects to them.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you include so we can answer your request.

2. Third-party services and sharing

Information needed to provide a feature may be processed by these services:

  • Expo: app updates, performance diagnostics and, where enabled, push notification delivery. Update requests may include app and runtime versions, operating-system information and update identifiers. See the Expo Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps and Firebase Cloud Messaging: maps, when configured, and Android remote notification delivery. These services may receive device, network and service-interaction information. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Lorem Picsum and its image-delivery providers: the Home examples load sample images from picsum.photos. Loading them sends network request information, including your IP address, to those providers. These requests do not upload the photos you select on your device.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this policy and may process visitor request information. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you choose to share content through your device’s share sheet, the receiving app or service handles that content under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect users, prevent abuse or defend legal rights.

3. Permissions and your choices

You can deny or revoke camera, microphone, location and notification permissions in your device’s system settings. The Application also provides controls for optional device features in Settings. Disabling a permission can make the related feature unavailable.

Disabling notifications does not disable app updates or diagnostics. The current Application does not provide a separate in-app diagnostics opt-out. You can stop further activity by closing or uninstalling the Application; this does not automatically delete information already received by a service provider.

This policy page contains no analytics scripts, advertising pixels or tracking cookies added by Hyrkul. Local app preferences are used for functionality.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Local preferences remain until you change them, clear the Application’s data where your device permits, or remove the Application. Operating-system backups may retain or restore app data under your device’s backup settings.
  • Location results are not persisted by the current location feature. The in-memory result is cleared when the feature is disabled or the app process ends.
  • Selected or captured images may remain in the Application’s local cache until cleared by the system or when app data is removed. Removing the Application does not delete originals in your photo library.
  • Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Provider-held diagnostics, delivery records and technical logs follow the relevant provider’s retention rules. The Application does not automatically erase those records when it is uninstalled.
